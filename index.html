🀄 台灣十六張麻將玩法（本遊戲版本）

牌與目標：使用萬、筒、索、東南西北、中發白及春夏秋冬、梅蘭菊竹，共 144 張。每位玩家平時持 16 張，莊家起手 17 張。胡牌需湊成 五組面子（順子／刻子／槓子）＋一對將眼，總共 17 張有效牌；槓子以一組面子計。

  1. 輪到您時：若手上是 16 張，按「摸牌」；摸到第 17 張後，選一張按「打出選中牌」。連點同張牌兩次也可以打出。
  2. 吃：只有上家（您左手順序的前一家，在本遊戲為電腦阿北）的捨牌可以吃；以同一花色相連三張組順子。
  3. 碰：他家打出您手上已有兩張的同一種牌，就可碰成刻子。
  4. 槓：四張相同牌可槓（含暗槓、碰後補槓或接他家捨牌明槓），槓完須補摸一張。
  5. 胡：您自摸或其他人打出的牌能完成牌型時，按「胡！」；若要放棄可按「過」。電腦會自動決定其行動。
  6. 花牌：春夏秋冬、梅蘭菊竹自動亮出並補花；本版花牌不計台。牌牆用完而未胡則流局。

智慧提示：「提示我該打哪張」根據牌型搭配與可聽牌數給出簡易建議；不保證最佳打法。

🀄 How to Play Taiwan 16-Tile Mahjong (This Game)

Tiles and goal: The set contains 144 tiles: Characters (Cracks), Dots, Bamboos, four Winds, three Dragons, and eight Flowers/Seasons. Each player normally holds 16 tiles; the dealer starts with 17. A winning hand has five melds (chows, pungs or kongs) plus one pair, representing 17 tiles. A kong counts as one meld.

  1. Take your turn: When you have 16 tiles, tap Draw, then select one tile and tap Discard. You can also tap the same tile twice to discard it.
  2. Chow: Claim a sequence of three consecutive tiles of the same suit only from the player immediately before you (the North computer in this game).
  3. Pung: Claim another player's discard when you already have two identical tiles, making three of a kind.
  4. Kong: Declare four identical tiles (concealed, from a discard, or by upgrading a pung); draw a replacement tile afterward.
  5. Win: Tap Win when your draw or another player's discard completes your hand. Tap Pass to decline a claim.
  6. Bonus tiles: Flowers and Seasons are revealed and automatically replaced. This simplified version does not award flower tai. If the wall empties without a win, the hand is a draw.

Hint: The Suggest a Discard button uses a simple hand-shape heuristic; it does not guarantee the strongest move.

📊 Tai and Payment Rules

計分設定（單位：分，非金錢） 放槍：只有放槍者支付底分＋台數×每台分值；自摸：另外三家各付一份。

門清自摸以「門清 1＋自摸 1＋加計 1＝3 台」計，不重複另加 3 台。暗槓不破門清。碰碰胡須五組刻子或槓子；清一色是只有一種數字花色；混一色（湊一色）是一種數字花色加字牌；大三元須中、發、白三組刻子或槓子，成立後不另算這三組各 1 台。

本遊戲採較嚴格的平胡：沒有字牌、沒有花牌，五組都為順子，必須胡他家捨牌並且是兩面聽；自摸、邊張、嵌張、單吊不計。各牌桌對平胡的定義可能不同。

放槍：只有放槍者一人支付「底分＋總台數×每台分值」，另外兩家付 0 分。自摸：其餘三家各支付一份；本遊戲閒家自摸時由莊家付款的那一份另加 1 台。莊家胡牌計莊家 1 台；閒家胡莊家捨牌加 1 台。以上為本遊戲採用的簡化計分，非所有牌桌共通規則。不計花台、連莊、包牌及其他未列牌型；不涉及真實金錢。

Concealed self-draw counts as 3 tai total (1 concealed + 1 self-draw + 1 additional), not 3 on top of those two. A concealed kong does not break concealment. All Pungs requires five triplet/kong melds. Full Flush uses only one numbered suit; Half Flush uses one suit plus Honors. Big Three Dragons requires triplets/kongs of Red, Green, and White Dragons; their individual dragon-triplet tai are then excluded.

This game uses a strict All Chows / Ping Hu rule: no Honors, no Flowers, five sequences, a discard win rather than self-draw, and a two-sided wait. Edge, inside and single-pair waits do not qualify. House rules vary.

Win on a discard (Ron): Only the discarding player pays base + total tai × points per tai; the two others pay 0. Self-draw (Tsumo): All three opponents pay one share each; when a non-dealer wins by self-draw, the dealer's payment includes an extra 1 tai in this game. A dealer winner gains 1 tai, and a non-dealer winning on the dealer's discard also gains 1 tai. These are simplified in-game house rules, not universal Mahjong rules. Flower bonuses, consecutive dealer bonuses, liability rules and unlisted special hands are not included. No real money is involved.

🔎 牌面辨識 / Tile Symbols

操作：點選手牌，再按「打出選中牌」；手機也可連點同一張牌兩次直接打出。只在輪到自己時摸牌；「吃／碰／槓／胡」符合條件時才可按；其他三家自動行動。花牌自動補牌，本版自動計算莊家、自摸、門清、碰碰胡、清一色、大三元、平胡、混一色（湊一色）與三元刻子；大三元不與三元刻重複計算。放槍者獨自支付「底＋台數」分數；自摸三家各付一份。花台、連莊與其餘特殊牌型尚未列入台數。手機重新整理時會盡量保留本局進度。各地桌規可能不同，請參考下方詳細計台說明。
